Interactive robotics study

The procedural particle field is adapted from ThreeUI Structure Flow by Meng To (MIT).
Source: https://github.com/MengTo/threeui/blob/main/src/shaders/structure-flow/structureFlowRenderer.ts
Changes: robot-study integration, yellow palette, point-count/performance controls, interactive scene composition.
The robot geometry is an original procedural visual study, not a CAD model of a specific project or a live hardware connection.
