Modifications for this portfolio
Prepared for Yoon Jeongmin's portfolio on 2026-08-18 directly from the C103 Team repository originals:
- Videos were re-encoded to H.264, yuv420p, maximum width 720 px, CRF 27, with faststart enabled.
- Audio tracks were removed for silent web demonstrations.
- WebP poster frames were newly extracted at approximately one second from each re-encoded video.
- Source JPEG photographs were resized to a maximum width of 1600 px and converted to WebP quality 84.
- File names were changed to concise English web paths.
